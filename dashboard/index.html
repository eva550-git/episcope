What-if planning controls

Move the controls to recalculate alerts and load a precomputed optimizer scenario.

Budget is a share of the estimated replacement cost of the four-week forecast demand. Equity floor is the minimum population-share allocation for each district. Alert threshold updates map markers and rankings. This demonstration does not replace public-health review.

Privacy: district-week counts from 1–4 are suppressed. National trend totals therefore show only visible cells and are explicitly partial; suppressed counts are not reconstructed by subtraction. The optimizer redistributes one national pool of existing stock; its recommendation replaces current district distribution rather than adding to it. Forecast, synthetic inventory, and optimizer recommendations are demonstration outputs, not validated medical or logistics advice.
